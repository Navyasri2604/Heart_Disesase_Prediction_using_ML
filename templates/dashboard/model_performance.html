Understanding the Metrics
Accuracy

Percentage of all predictions that were correct. Overall correctness.

Precision

Of patients predicted positive, how many actually had heart disease? Reduces false alarms.

Recall

Of patients who actually had disease, how many did we catch? Critical in healthcare — reduces missed cases.

F1 Score

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Best balance metric for imbalanced medical datasets.
